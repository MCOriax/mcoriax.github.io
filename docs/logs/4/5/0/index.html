Change Log — 4.5.0

Permalink for version 4.5.0.

4.5.0 Latest 2026-08-02 · Identities go by name

An identity stops introducing itself by its class and starts going by its name. The item you carry, and every alternative identity head in the Player Identity GUI, is now titled with that identity's alias, with its profession and level moved down into the lore. Identity UUIDs are gone from the menus entirely.

ℹ️

Nothing to turn on and nothing to migrate. No schema change and no new configuration key. In this release an identity item already in circulation is re-titled on its next level up, which is the only moment the item is rebuilt. 4.5.1 adds the two refreshes that were missing here, so an item is also re-titled the moment an alias is claimed and whenever the identity is equipped.

Added Identities are named by their alias

  • An identity item is titled with the alias claimed for it, in a colour derived from the identity itself, so it keeps the same colour across relogs instead of being recoloured each time it is rebuilt.
  • Its profession and level sit below in the lore, and the level shows as MAX once the profession's cap is reached.
  • An identity that has not claimed an alias yet is titled with its profession name, so an item is never left blank.
  • See Commands & Permissions for how to claim an alias.

Added The same in the Player Identity GUI

  • Every alternative identity head is titled with that identity's alias and carries its profession and level in the lore, matching the item.
  • The primary identity head keeps its fixed "Primary Identity" name, because the base player identity always goes by your Minecraft name and never carries an alias. Its profession and level are shown in its lore all the same.
  • See GUI System for the full menu reference.

Changed Identity UUIDs are no longer shown

  • The UUID: lore line is gone from both the alternative and the primary identity heads.
  • Which identity a head stands for is carried in the item's persistent data instead, so clicking one resolves the same way with nothing printed on screen.

Fixed Renaming an identity item no longer breaks it

  • Levelling up used to update an item by reading the level back out of its own display name. A player who renamed their identity item silently stopped getting level updates on it.
  • The item is now rebuilt from the identity's stored data, and nothing anywhere reads an item's name or lore to decide what it is.
  • Renaming an identity item or clearing its lore is therefore purely cosmetic: equipping, levelling, and everything else keep working, and the text is rewritten the next time the identity is rendered.

Note Server owners

  • Drop the new jar in and restart. No schema change, no configuration change, and nothing to migrate on any backend, the central server included.
  • Behaviour is identical on SpigotMC, PaperMC, and Folia.
  • Opening the Player Identity GUI costs no extra database round trip: the alias and level are read in the lookup pass that already fetched each head's texture.
